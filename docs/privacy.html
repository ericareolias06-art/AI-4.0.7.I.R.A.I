Irai Tec Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

App and developer: Irai Tec. Privacy contact: areobooks@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how Irai Tec ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Irai Tec Android application and related account services.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, a securely hashed password (we do not store your plain-text password), and account identifiers.
  • Subscription information: Premium status, subscription identifiers, subscription status, and related billing references used to manage access.
  • Voice and speech: Irai uses microphone access for wake-word detection and voice interaction. The app's speech-recognition feature converts spoken commands into text. The recognized text may be sent to our server as part of your request. Android's speech-recognition service may process audio according to the provider and device settings. We do not intentionally send raw microphone recordings to the Ollama AI endpoint.
  • AI messages: Text you submit, and any recent conversation context included with a request, is transmitted through our server to our Ollama-powered AI service so it can generate a response. The AI chat endpoint does not save chat transcripts as account history. Request data is processed to return the response and is not intentionally retained as conversation history.
  • Document scanning: If you choose Scan a Page, the camera app captures a page image, which is saved in your device's Pictures/IraiScans folder. Irai uses on-device text recognition to extract the words. The extracted text, not the page photo itself, is sent through our server to the AI service so Irai can explain the document. Do not scan sensitive information unless you are comfortable sending its text for AI processing.
  • Guided Assistance: If you enable Irai Guided Assistance in Android Accessibility settings, Irai can read visible on-screen text when you explicitly ask for help. When you request screen guidance, the visible text excerpt is sent through our server to the AI service to generate an explanation. Irai does not intentionally collect screen content in the background. If you ask Irai to tap a control or enter text, those actions are performed locally on your device after you approve the confirmation dialog.
  • Technical information: Our hosting and security providers may process routine connection and server logs, such as IP address, request time, and diagnostic information, to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, provide Irai's voice and AI features, manage Premium access, process account deletion, secure the service, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate Irai Tec. Account and AI requests are handled by our application hosting and database providers. Text submitted for AI responses, document text extracted from scans, and on-screen text you ask Irai to explain are routed to our Ollama AI service through Cloudflare infrastructure. Page photos used for scanning are saved locally in your device's Pictures/IraiScans folder; Irai does not intentionally upload the photo itself. We use Stripe for subscription checkout and billing. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share AI messages with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, or our rights.

4. Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Irai Tec does not receive or store your full payment-card number. We may receive and store Stripe customer, subscription, price, and subscription-status identifiers to manage your Premium account. Stripe may retain transaction and billing records under its own legal and retention requirements.

5. Retention

We retain account information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. AI chat requests are processed to generate replies and are not stored as chat history by the AI chat endpoint. Routine technical logs and provider records may be retained for limited periods under the relevant provider's policies. Some billing records may be retained by Stripe independently of your Irai Tec account.

6. Account deletion

You can request deletion from within the Irai Tec app (when the account deletion control is available in your app version) or use our public account deletion page. Deletion removes your Irai Tec account and associated account records, including the email address, password hash, subscription references, and promo-redemption record. If you have an active Stripe subscription, the deletion process attempts to cancel it first. Stripe may retain transaction records it is legally or operationally required to keep. Contact areobooks@gmail.com if you cannot access your account or need help.

7. Children's privacy

Irai Tec is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect account information, including password hashing, HTTPS connections, and session-based authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Future features

Future versions may offer optional on-device memory or personalization. This policy will be updated before such features are introduced to explain what is stored, where it is stored, and how users can manage or delete it. The current version does not describe an active cloud-based personal-memory feature.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Irai Tec changes. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, email areobooks@gmail.com.
